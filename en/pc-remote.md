# PC Remote Guide

Control your computer from your phone, turn it into a home theater, and operate it from the couch.

## Before You Start

### 1. Download the Desktop App

Download the version for your operating system:
- **Windows:** Windows 10/11
- **Mac:** macOS 10.15 or later

> Download: [Official Website](https://www.vonchange.com)

### 2. Install and Run It

1. Install the downloaded app.
2. Launch it and keep it running in the background.

### 3. Use the Same Network

- ✅ Your phone and computer are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- ✅ The desktop app is running.

## Steps

### Step 1: Connect a Device

1. Open Marmot TV Remote and tap **PC Remote**.
2. Tap the **Discovery** button 🔍 in the upper-right corner.
3. The app automatically finds nearby computers running the desktop app.
4. Select your computer to start controlling it.

### Step 2: Start Controlling

After connecting, you can control your computer from your phone.

## Controls

### 🖱️ Air Mouse

Tap the **Air Mouse** button in the bottom toolbar:

- **Move the pointer:** Slide your finger across the touchpad.
- **Click:** Tap the touchpad.
- **Vertical scroll:** Swipe on the vertical scroll area on the right.
- **Horizontal scroll:** Swipe on the horizontal scroll area at the bottom.
- **Right-click:** Tap the right side of the horizontal scroll area.

### ⌨️ Keyboard Input

- **Text input:** Type with your phone keyboard.
- **Shortcuts:** Use common keyboard shortcuts.

### 📺 Video Resources

- **Video playback:** Browse and cast videos from the browser.
- **Playback controls:** Play, pause, and seek.
- **Full screen:** Enter full screen with one tap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why can I not find my computer?

Check that:
- The desktop app is running.
- Your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Your computer firewall allows the connection.

### Q: Which operating systems are supported?

- Windows 10/11
- macOS 10.15 or later
