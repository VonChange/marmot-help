# Photo Cast User Guide

Cast photos from your phone gallery to your TV and share precious memories with family and friends.

## Preparation

### 1. Ensure Device Connection

- ✅ Phone and TV are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**
- ✅ TV supports DLNA (most smart TVs do)
- ✅ DLNA service is enabled on TV

### 2. Open Photo Cast

Tap the **Photo Cast** button on the app home page to enter the photo casting page.

## Usage Steps

### Step 1: Select Device

The app will automatically search for nearby DLNA devices. Select your TV from the device list.

**Tips**:
- If you can't see your device, check if DLNA is enabled on your TV
- Make sure phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
- Tap the refresh button to search again

### Step 2: Select Photos

After selecting a device, the app will display your phone gallery. You can:

- **Browse albums**: Swipe left/right to view different albums
- **Select photos**: Tap a photo to select it
- **Multi-select**: Long press a photo to enter multi-select mode

### Step 3: Start Casting

After selecting photos, tap the **Start Cast** button and the photos will appear on your TV.

**Casting Controls**:
- **Previous/Next**: Swipe left/right to switch photos
- **Pause/Resume**: Pause or resume playback
- **Stop Cast**: Stop casting and return

## Features

### 📸 Auto Play

After selecting multiple photos, you can enable auto play to automatically switch between photos.

### ⏱️ Interval Setting

Set display time for each photo, from 3 to 30 seconds.

### 🔄 Loop Playback

Enable loop playback to continuously play photos until manually stopped.

## FAQ

### Q: Can't find device?

**A**: Please check:
1. Phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
2. DLNA is enabled on TV
3. Try restarting TV and phone
4. Tap refresh button to search again

### Q: Casting failed?

**A**: Try:
1. Check network connection stability
2. Re-select device
3. Restart the app
4. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for more help

### Q: Photo appears blurry?

**A**: Photos are cast at original resolution. If your TV has lower resolution, it may affect display quality. This is normal.
