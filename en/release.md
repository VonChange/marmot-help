# Release Notes

Update history for the international **Marmot TV Remote** app. Newest first.

---

## Version 1.0

First international release.

### TV Remote
- **Air Mouse:** Use your phone as a touchpad — swipe to move the cursor, tap to click, two-finger drag to scroll the TV screen.
- **Classic remote:** Directional pad, OK, Back, Home, volume, and common shortcuts.
- **Real-time text input:** Type on your phone and send text to the TV instantly for search, sign-in, and passwords.
- **Device discovery:** Find Google TV / Android TV devices on the same Wi-Fi, with Install Service, Repair, and manual IP entry when needed.

### ADB tools
- Install and uninstall apps.
- File transfer and device info.
- Screenshots, screen recording, and common utilities (including set default launcher where supported).

### Casting
- Cast local photos and videos from your phone to DLNA-compatible TVs.

### Account and subscription
- StoreKit yearly subscription and lifetime purchase.
- Restore Purchases after changing phones.
