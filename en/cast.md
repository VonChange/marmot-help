# Photo and Video Casting Guide

Cast photos and videos from your phone to a TV and share your favorite moments with family and friends.

## Before You Start

### 1. Check the Device Connection

- ✅ Your phone and TV are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- ✅ Your TV supports DLNA. Most smart TVs do.
- ✅ DLNA is enabled on the TV.

### 2. Open Casting

Tap **Photo Cast** or **Video Cast** on the app home screen.

## Steps

### Step 1: Select a Device

The app automatically searches for nearby DLNA devices. Select your TV from the device list.

**Tips:**
- If no device appears, check that DLNA is enabled on the TV.
- Make sure your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Tap Refresh to search again.

### Step 2: Cast Content

After selecting a device, browse your photos or video library and **tap a photo or video to cast it directly to the TV**.

- **Photo Cast:** Tap a photo to display it immediately. **Touch and hold a photo** to enter slideshow casting mode and automatically show multiple photos in sequence.
- **Video Cast:** Tap a video to start playing it on the TV.

### Video Playback Controls

After casting a video, use your phone to control playback:

- **Play/Pause:** Start or pause playback.
- **Seek:** Drag the progress bar to a specific position.
- **Volume:** Adjust the TV volume.
- **Playback Speed:** Select 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x speed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What should I do if I cannot find my TV?

Make sure that:
- Your phone and TV are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- **DLNA** is enabled on the TV.

### Q: Which formats are supported?

- **Photos:** Common formats such as JPG, PNG, and GIF.
- **Videos:** Common formats such as MP4, MKV, and AVI.
