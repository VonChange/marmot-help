# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Q: Is Wi-Fi required?

Yes. All features require your phone and target device to be connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.

### Q: Why can I not find my device?

Check that:
- Your phone and target device are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- TV Remote: **ADB debugging** is enabled on the TV.
- PC Remote: The desktop app is running.
- Casting: The TV supports **DLNA** and DLNA is enabled.

### Q: Which permissions does the app require?

- **Network:** Search for and connect to devices.
- **Photos and storage:** Access photos and videos on your phone.
- **Location (Android):** Required by Android when scanning for Wi-Fi devices.

## TV Remote

### Q: How do I enable ADB on my TV?

The steps vary by brand. See [How to Enable ADB Debugging](adb.md).

### Q: What do I need to do for the first connection?

1. Enable ADB debugging on the TV.
2. On your phone, open **TV Remote** and tap the ADB button in the upper-right corner.
3. Select the TV and approve the authorization prompt on the TV.
4. For future connections, tap the **Discovery** button and select the TV.

### Q: How do I change the default launcher on my TV?

Open **TV Remote > Utilities > Set Default Launcher** and select a launcher app. Some TVs can switch silently; if that fails, open the system chooser and confirm on the TV. If only the system launcher is listed, install another launcher app first. The original launcher cannot be forcibly disabled on unsupported devices.

## PC Remote

### Q: Where can I download the desktop app?

Download the version for your operating system from [www.vonchange.com](https://www.vonchange.com).

### Q: Why can I not find my computer?

- Make sure the desktop app is running.
- Keep your phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Check the firewall settings on your computer.

---

## Casting

### Q: Why can I not find my TV?

- Make sure the TV supports DLNA and DLNA is enabled.
- Keep your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network.

### Q: Which formats are supported?

- **Photos:** JPG, PNG, GIF, and other common formats.
- **Videos:** MP4, MKV, AVI, and other common formats.
