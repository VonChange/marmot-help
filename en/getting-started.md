# Getting Started

Welcome to Marmot TV Assistant! This guide will help you get started quickly.

## What is Marmot TV Assistant?

Marmot TV Assistant is a powerful mobile app that lets you easily control your TV and PC with your phone, and cast photos and videos from your phone to your TV.

## Main Features

### 📸 Photo Cast
Cast photos from your phone gallery to your TV and share precious memories with family and friends.

### 🎬 Video Cast
Cast videos from your phone to your TV for a big-screen viewing experience.

### 📺 TV Remote
Control your smart TV with your phone - no need to search for the remote.

### 💻 PC Remote
Control your computer with your phone, quick and convenient.

## First Time Setup

### 1. Ensure Devices are on the Same Network

All features require your phone and TV/PC to be connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

**How to check**:
- Phone: Settings → Wi-Fi, check the connected Wi-Fi name
- TV: Settings → Network, check the connected Wi-Fi name
- PC: Check the Wi-Fi icon in taskbar, confirm the connected Wi-Fi name

**Important**: Make sure all devices are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.

### 2. Choose a Feature

After opening the app, you'll see these feature options:

- **Photo Cast**: Tap to enter photo casting
- **Video Cast**: Tap to enter video casting
- **TV Remote**: Tap to enter TV remote control
- **PC Remote**: Tap to enter PC remote control

### 3. Connect to Device

Based on your chosen feature, the app will automatically search for nearby devices:

- **Casting features**: Search for DLNA-compatible TVs or casting devices
- **Remote features**: Search for TVs or PCs with MarmotX app installed

Select the device you want to connect to and start using it.
