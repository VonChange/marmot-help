# TV Remote User Guide

Control your smart TV with your phone - no need to search for the remote.

## Preparation

### 1. Install MarmotX

**MarmotX** app must be installed on your TV to use the remote control feature.

**Installation**:
1. Search for "MarmotX" in your TV's app store
2. Download and install MarmotX app
3. Open MarmotX app and make sure it's running

### 2. Ensure Device Connection

- ✅ Phone and TV are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**
- ✅ MarmotX app is running on TV
- ✅ Phone and TV are on the same local network

### 3. Open TV Remote

Tap the **TV Remote** button on the app home page to enter the TV remote page.

## Usage Steps

### Step 1: Connect to Device

The app will automatically search for nearby TVs with MarmotX installed. Select your TV from the device list.

**Tips**:
- If you can't see your device, check if MarmotX is running
- Make sure phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
- Tap the refresh button to search again

### Step 2: Start Controlling

Once connected, you can start controlling your TV with your phone.

## Control Features

### 🎮 D-Pad Control

- **Up/Down/Left/Right**: Navigate TV focus
- **OK Button**: Confirm selection

### 📺 Quick Functions

- **Back**: Go back to previous screen
- **Home**: Return to TV home screen
- **Menu**: Open menu
- **Volume +/-**: Adjust volume
- **Mute**: Mute/unmute

### 🔍 App Management

- **Open App**: Quickly open apps on TV
- **App List**: View installed apps
- **Recent**: View recently used apps

## FAQ

### Q: Can't find device?

**A**: Please check:
1. MarmotX app is installed and running on TV
2. Phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
3. MarmotX app is running properly
4. Try restarting MarmotX app
5. Tap refresh button to search again

### Q: Connection failed?

**A**: Try:
1. Check network connection stability
2. Restart MarmotX app
3. Restart phone and TV
4. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for more help

### Q: Control not responding?

**A**: Please check:
1. MarmotX app is running
2. Network connection is stable
3. Try reconnecting to device
4. Restart MarmotX app

### Q: Which TVs are supported?

**A**: All smart TVs with MarmotX installed are supported. MarmotX supports Android TV and most smart TV systems.
