# Video Cast User Guide

Cast videos from your phone to your TV for a big-screen viewing experience.

## Preparation

### 1. Ensure Device Connection

- ✅ Phone and TV are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**
- ✅ TV supports DLNA (most smart TVs do)
- ✅ DLNA service is enabled on TV

### 2. Open Video Cast

Tap the **Video Cast** button on the app home page to enter the video casting page.

## Usage Steps

### Step 1: Select Device

The app will automatically search for nearby DLNA devices. Select your TV from the device list.

**Tips**:
- If you can't see your device, check if DLNA is enabled on your TV
- Make sure phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
- Tap the refresh button to search again

### Step 2: Select Video

After selecting a device, the app will display your phone video library. You can:

- **Browse videos**: Swipe left/right to view different videos
- **Select video**: Tap a video to select it
- **View info**: Tap video to view detailed information

### Step 3: Start Casting

After selecting a video, tap the **Start Cast** button and the video will play on your TV.

**Casting Controls**:
- **Play/Pause**: Control video playback
- **Progress**: Drag the progress bar to seek
- **Volume**: Adjust TV volume
- **Speed**: Support 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x playback speeds
- **Stop Cast**: Stop casting and return

## Features

### 🎬 Full Playback Control

Supports play, pause, seek, volume control and other complete playback controls.

### ⚡ Speed Control

Support 0.5x to 2x playback speeds to meet different viewing needs.

### 📱 Phone Control

After casting, you can control video playback with your phone without using the TV remote.

## FAQ

### Q: Can't find device?

**A**: Please check:
1. Phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network
2. DLNA is enabled on TV
3. Try restarting TV and phone
4. Tap refresh button to search again

### Q: Video playback is laggy?

**A**: Video lag may be due to network issues. Try:
1. Check Wi-Fi signal strength
2. Move phone and TV closer to router
3. Turn off other devices using the network
4. Try lower video resolution (if supported)

### Q: Casting failed?

**A**: Try:
1. Check network connection stability
2. Re-select device
3. Restart the app
4. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for more help

### Q: What video formats are supported?

**A**: Common video formats like MP4, AVI, MOV are supported. If a format is not supported, please convert it first.
